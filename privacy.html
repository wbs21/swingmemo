SwingMemo is a tennis spending journal. This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices available to you.

What SwingMemo collects

SwingMemo does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data.

  • No account is required.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs are included.
  • No usage data is sent to a server.
  • The developer does not operate a server that receives your app data.

Data stored on your device

SwingMemo stores the information you enter so the journal can function:

  • Expense entries, notes, ratings, tags, dates and amounts
  • Photos you choose to attach
  • Budget settings
  • Local daily snapshots of your entries
  • Purchase state provided by Apple's StoreKit

Attached photos are copied into the app only after processing: longest edge 1600 pixels, JPEG quality 0.75, orientation normalized, and metadata removed. Original camera or photo-library files are not copied into the app.

Backups and exports

You can export a full backup file (.swingmemo), a CSV file or a PDF report. These files are created on your device and leave the app only when you choose to share them through the system share sheet.

Backups contain private notes and photos. PDF reports may contain notes but do not include photos. Export files are not encrypted by SwingMemo. Store them somewhere safe.

Optional iCloud backup

If you choose, SwingMemo can write an additional backup file to your private iCloud Drive container. The developer cannot access this file. It is stored under your Apple ID and counts toward your iCloud storage.

You can delete the iCloud backup inside the app at any time. iCloud backup is optional; the app remains functional offline and without iCloud.

Purchases

SwingMemo Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. Payment information is handled by Apple; SwingMemo does not receive or store it. Purchase entitlement is validated through StoreKit.

Photos and camera

SwingMemo requests camera and photo-library access only when you choose to attach a photo. Photos stay on your device and are included in backups only when you export one.

Children's privacy

SwingMemo does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data deletion

Deleting an entry removes it from the app. Attached photos may be retained temporarily while referenced by a daily snapshot, then cleaned up automatically. Uninstalling the app removes all local app data. Export a full backup before uninstalling if you want to keep your journal.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be included with a future app update and published on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to the SwingMemo support address.

Protect your journal

Your entries are stored locally on your iPhone. Before deleting the app, changing devices or resetting your phone, create a full SwingMemo backup and verify that it can be opened from Files.

Daily snapshots are a recovery aid, not a replacement for a complete exported backup because they retain a limited history.

Quick help

Common questions

Restore SwingMemo Pro

Open the Pro purchase screen and choose “Restore Purchases.” Use the same Apple ID that bought Pro. A current internet connection is required for StoreKit to verify the purchase.

Back up before uninstalling

Create a full .swingmemo backup, save it somewhere outside the app, and test the file before uninstalling SwingMemo.

Restore a backup

Use the backup and restore option from the app. Restores merge missing records and do not overwrite existing entries with the same identifier.

Camera or photo access

Open iOS Settings, find SwingMemo and enable Camera or Photos access. SwingMemo only requests access when you choose to attach an image.

Photos look different after saving

SwingMemo crops the image, limits its longest edge to 1600 pixels, compresses it as JPEG and removes metadata to save space and protect privacy.

Report a bug

Open a GitHub issue with your device, iOS version, and the shortest sequence of steps that reproduces the problem. Do not attach a full backup unless requested.

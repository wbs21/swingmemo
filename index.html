Included free · Ownership

Keep control of your journal

There is no account or developer server. Your data leaves the app only when you choose an export or backup destination.

  • Seven-day snapshots A local snapshot is created on each active day; the latest seven active days are retained.
  • Full backup and restore Export a single .swingmemo file with records, settings and compressed photos.
  • Merge-safe restore Missing records are imported, duplicates are skipped and existing records are not overwritten.
  • Optional iCloud Drive backup Write a backup to your private iCloud container only when you choose to.
  • CSV export Open the journal in a spreadsheet for personal accounting or sorting.
  • Privacy-first storage No login, ads, analytics, tracking SDKs or developer-operated user-data server.

Good to know

Questions before your first entry

What is SwingMemo?

SwingMemo is a tennis expense journal for iPhone. It records court fees, coaching, balls, rackets, apparel, shoes, gear and other tennis costs, then offers optional Pro tools for understanding the season.

Does SwingMemo require an account?

No. The app works without registration and stores your journal locally on your iPhone.

Can I back up my entries?

Yes. You can create a complete SwingMemo backup, keep seven days of local snapshots, use optional private iCloud Drive backup, or export a CSV. See the support page for restore guidance.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. SwingMemo Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. There is no recurring subscription.

What happens to attached photos?

Photos are cropped, resized to a maximum 1600-pixel edge, compressed as JPEG and stripped of metadata before SwingMemo stores them.

What does SwingMemo Pro include?

Pro adds weekly, monthly and yearly trends, category mix and unit costs, monthly and custom-season budgets, event and training-focus summaries, Year in Review and branded PDF expense reports.

Does SwingMemo support iPad?

SwingMemo is designed for iPhone and requires iOS 17 or later. The current release does not include a dedicated iPad layout.
